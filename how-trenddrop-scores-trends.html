Methodology

How TrendDrop thinks about a trend score.

Popularity alone is not enough. TrendDrop's scoring idea is built around whether attention is moving, whether people are looking for the topic, how fresh it is and how crowded the creator landscape may be.

Growth speed

Growth asks whether attention is accelerating. A topic moving quickly can be more actionable than a larger topic that has been flat for months.

Search interest

Search interest is useful when viewers are actively trying to understand, compare or solve something. It can indicate demand that is more intentional than passive exposure.

Social activity

Conversation volume, sharing and creator activity can indicate that a topic is entering broader awareness. Social activity is interpreted alongside growth rather than treated as proof of future performance.

Freshness

Freshness asks how recently the opportunity changed. Product launches, new formats, news events, updates and new audience behaviors can temporarily make older content less useful.

Competition

Competition works in the opposite direction. If a topic is already covered by a very large number of strong, similar videos, the opportunity may be weaker even when overall interest is high.

A score is a filter, not a prediction

A trend score is designed to help prioritize research. It is not a forecast of exact views or revenue. Creative quality, audience fit, retention and platform distribution can overwhelm any trend signal.

Transparency: The current TrendDrop homepage is a product prototype and includes demonstration trend values while live data integrations are being developed. The scoring framework describes the product methodology and should not be interpreted as audited real-time market data.
