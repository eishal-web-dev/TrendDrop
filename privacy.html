Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026.

TrendDrop is a creator-focused software product operated as part of Ashes. This policy explains the information that may be collected when you use the TrendDrop website and how it may be used.

Information you provide

If you contact us, create an account in future product versions, submit payment information through an external payment provider, or send feedback, we may receive information such as your name, email address, message content and account-related information.

Usage and technical information

We may collect privacy-friendly usage information such as pages viewed, browser type, device type, approximate region, referring page and interaction events. We use this information to improve the product, understand performance and diagnose errors.

Cookies and advertising

TrendDrop may use cookies or similar technologies for essential functionality, analytics, fraud prevention and advertising. If Google AdSense or another advertising provider is enabled, those providers may use cookies or identifiers to serve and measure ads in accordance with their own policies and applicable consent requirements.

Payments

Payment card or wallet processing may be handled by third-party payment providers. TrendDrop does not intend to store full card numbers on its own servers. Third-party processors handle payment information under their own privacy and security terms.

How information is used

  • Provide and improve TrendDrop.
  • Respond to support or feedback.
  • Measure site performance and usage.
  • Prevent abuse and security incidents.
  • Operate billing or subscriptions where applicable.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

Data retention

We aim to keep personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, legal requirements, dispute resolution or security.

Your choices

You may contact us to ask about data associated with you or to request correction or deletion where applicable. Browser and device settings may also allow you to control cookies and tracking technologies.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent through the TrendDrop contact page.
