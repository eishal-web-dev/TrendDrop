Daily workflow

What should I post today?

A better answer starts with your audience, not with whatever happens to be loudest on your feed.

Start with one platform and one audience

If you try to make one idea work equally well on TikTok, Reels and Shorts, the result often becomes generic. Choose the platform you want to win today and picture the person who should care about the video.

Look for movement, not just popularity

A topic can be huge and still be a poor opportunity because thousands of creators are already posting the same angle. Look for subjects that are gaining attention quickly, especially when the conversation is becoming more specific.

Choose the narrow version of the trend

“AI” is too broad. “AI agents automating repetitive office tasks” gives you a clearer promise. “Fitness” is broad. “Silent apartment workouts” gives you a reason for a specific audience to stop scrolling.

Write the hook before the whole script

Your first sentence should make the value of the video obvious. Good hooks create curiosity without hiding the topic. A useful test is whether a viewer can tell what they will learn within the first few seconds.

Use a simple 30-second structure

  1. 0–3 seconds: state the tension, surprise or promise.
  2. 4–24 seconds: deliver the examples, demonstration or explanation.
  3. 25–30 seconds: close with the result, takeaway or next action.

Do not confuse “trending” with “guaranteed”

Even a strong rising topic can underperform if the angle is weak, the opening is slow or the audience does not care. Trend data should help you choose what to test; it cannot guarantee distribution.

A five-minute daily decision

Pick region, niche and platform. Compare freshness and competition. Choose one specific angle. Write the hook. Film. The purpose of a trend tool is to shorten the decision, not create another research habit.
