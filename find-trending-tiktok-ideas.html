TikTok strategy

How to find trending TikTok ideas before everyone copies them.

The goal is not to discover the biggest trend. It is to catch a useful angle while attention is still accelerating.

Watch for topic expansion

Many strong TikTok opportunities begin when a broad topic branches into smaller use cases. Instead of copying “morning routine,” look for “morning routine for night-shift workers” or another audience-specific version.

Compare velocity with competition

A fast-growing topic with moderate creator competition can be more useful than a giant trend full of near-identical videos. Ask two questions: is attention rising, and can you still contribute something distinct?

Use comments as idea research

Comments reveal what viewers still want explained. Repeated questions are often better content prompts than copying the original creator's format. Turn unanswered questions into your next angle.

Make the first seconds concrete

A TikTok hook should quickly communicate who the video is for and why it matters. Specific numbers, outcomes, mistakes, demonstrations and before/after contrasts are easier to understand than vague suspense.

Validate against your niche

A trend can be globally popular and irrelevant to your audience. Before filming, connect the idea to a problem, desire or curiosity your existing viewers already care about.

Use trends as raw material

The strongest creators rarely reproduce a trend unchanged. They add expertise, humor, a local perspective, an experiment, a comparison or a personal result. That is what turns a trend into original content.
