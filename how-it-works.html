Method

From noisy signals to one useful idea.

TrendDrop is designed to reduce a large set of possible topics into something a creator can understand and film quickly.

1. Start with context

A trend only matters if it matches the creator. Region, niche and platform change what “relevant” means, so TrendDrop begins there rather than with a global list.

2. Compare momentum

The scoring model considers five dimensions: growth speed, search interest, social activity, freshness and competition. High momentum with lower competition is more interesting than raw popularity alone.

3. Turn the signal into a creator angle

A trend name is not a video. TrendDrop converts the selected topic into a usable angle, opening hook, short structure, caption and hashtags. The goal is to reduce blank-page time, not replace creator judgment.

4. Show the reasoning

The score breakdown is visible so users can see why an opportunity ranks well. A fresh topic with moderate search demand may be useful for a different reason than a mature topic with very high social activity.

Current product note: TrendDrop is still evolving. Some signals in the live prototype are demonstration data while real multi-source collection is being built. We do not present demo values as guaranteed real-time market measurements.

5. Keep editorial responsibility with the creator

No trend engine can guarantee reach. Audience fit, originality, retention, production quality and platform distribution still determine performance. TrendDrop is a decision-support tool.
